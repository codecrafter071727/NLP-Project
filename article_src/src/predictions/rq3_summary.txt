RQ3 official evaluation

Protocol
Rows are read from datastore/frjudge.csv.
Each trait is measured from the original sentence and its simplification.
The same pair always produces the same trait value.
Correlation with the human rating is estimated on the training split (n=186) and checked on the test split (n=90).
A trait is reliable when three things hold: the identical holdout sits on the expected side of the unrelated holdout,
the training and test correlations have the same sign, and the test correlation has p < 0.05.
This dataset has no user search logs. The traits describe how a legal sentence was simplified.

What each trait measures
  overlap: Share of words that appear in both texts.
  words_kept: Share of original words that remain in the simplification.
  words_from_original: Share of simplification words that come from the original.
  length_closeness: 1 when the two texts have the same number of words, lower when one is much longer.
  length_change: Simplification length divided by original length. 1 means no change.
  sentence_split: Simplification sentences divided by original sentences. Above 1 means the sentence was split.
  shorter_words: Average original word length minus average simplification word length.
